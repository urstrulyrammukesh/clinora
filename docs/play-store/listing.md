Clinora helps you find the right doctor, fast, anywhere in the world.

🩺 FIND DOCTORS NEAR YOU
• See hospitals, clinics, doctors, dentists, labs and pharmacies near you on a live 3D map
• Know who is OPEN RIGHT NOW, with address, phone and directions
• Search any city, country or PIN code worldwide
• Find doctors listed on official university and medical college websites (AIIMS, IIT and NIT hospitals and more)

📹 VIDEO CONSULTATIONS & MESSAGES
• Book in-clinic visits or video consultations
• Message doctors and video call them, like WhatsApp
• Doctors show their online hours, so you know when they are available

💬 AI HEALTH ASSISTANT, 24×7
• Ask health questions any time of day or night, privately and without judgement
• Book appointments by chatting in your own language
• Emotional support when you feel low, with the right helpline for your country

🍔 FOOD SCAN
• Take a photo of any food or packet label
• Learn if it is OK for YOU, based on your allergies, conditions and diet
• Calories, sugar, salt, allergens and a healthier swap

⭐ HONEST REVIEWS
• Read and write reviews with stars, emoji and stickers
• See photos of a doctor's clinic and work

🚑 EMERGENCY HELP
• One tap to the ambulance and helpline numbers for your country

📚 FREE MEDICAL BOOKS
• Read free textbooks and health guides from around the world

🌍 139 LANGUAGES
• Hindi, English, Spanish, Arabic, French and many more

Important: Clinora gives general health information and helps you reach doctors. It does not replace a doctor's advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

For doctors and clinics: create a free profile, set your online hours, add photos of your work, reply to reviews and receive bookings, messages and video calls.
