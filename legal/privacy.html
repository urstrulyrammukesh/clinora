3. Services we share data with

We use these services only to run Clinora:

  • Anthropic (AI assistant, translations, Food Scan, university doctor search)
  • Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal (payments)
  • OpenStreetMap, Nominatim, Overpass, OpenFreeMap (maps, addresses, nearby places)
  • Google STUN servers (to help connect video calls)
  • Open Library and Google Books (free book search: only your search words)
  • Google, Facebook (Meta) and X (only if you choose to log in with them)
  • Our email service (only to send you a password reset link, and reminder emails you switched on)
  • Our hosting provider (stores the server and database)

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Health information

Some information may relate to your health, for example messages to a doctor or your bookings. We use it only to provide the service you asked for. Clinora's AI does not give medical diagnoses and is not a replacement for a doctor.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, work photos, reviews, messages and follows straight away. Booking and payment records may be kept as long as the law requires (for example for tax and accounting).

6. Your rights

You can see and change your details in your account, download a copy by asking us, and delete your account at any time: how to delete your account. You can also contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} to correct or remove data, or to complain. If you are in India, this is also our grievance contact under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, hashed passwords, secure login cookies, rate limits and checks on every payment. No system is 100% secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

Clinora is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use it only with a parent's or guardian's consent.
