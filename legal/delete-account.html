Delete your Clinora account

You can delete your account and its data yourself, at any time, in the app or on the website.

Delete it yourself (takes 1 minute)

  1. Open Clinora and log in.
  2. Open the ☰ menu and tap your name to go to Your account.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type your password to confirm. If you signed up with Google, Facebook or X and never set a password, type DELETE instead.
Go to my account

Or ask us

If you can't log in, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, name, email, city and password
  • Your profile photo
  • Doctors: your public profile, online hours, work photos, demo videos, verification details and certificate photo, and the reviews, video reviews and follows on your profile
  • Your messages, reviews, video reviews, notifications, reminders, reports you sent, people you blocked and the doctors you follow

What may be kept

Records of bookings and payments may be kept for as long as the law requires (for example tax and accounting rules), and then deleted. Settings saved on your own device (language, colours, Food Scan profile) stay on your device until you clear the app's data.
